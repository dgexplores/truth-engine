# Turmeric Neem

## Turmeric patent revoked
The US patent on turmeric wound-healing was revoked after Indian traditional knowledge records proved prior use. In plain words: ancient texts beat a new patent claim on turmeric.

## Neem patent revoked
The European patent on neem fungicide was revoked on similar traditional-knowledge evidence.
