# Designs

## Sec 4 — Prohibition of registration
A design which is not new or original, or has been disclosed to the public, shall not be registered. In plain words: packaging design must be new to get protection.

## Sec 22 — Piracy of registered design
Imitation of a registered design for sale of any article is piracy.
