# Patents Act

## Sec 3(p) — Not patentable (TK bar)
An invention which in effect is traditional knowledge or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components is not an invention. A classical churna recipe passed down by a grandmother is traditional knowledge and falls under this bar.

## Sec 3(d) — Mere discovery
The mere discovery of a scientific principle or the formulation of an abstract theory or discovery of any living thing or non-living substance occurring in nature is not an invention.

## Sec 2(1)(j) — Invention defined
Invention means a new product or process involving an inventive step and capable of industrial application.
