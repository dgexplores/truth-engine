# Drugs

## Sec 18 — Manufacture and sale
No person shall manufacture or sell any drug or cosmetic except under a licence. In plain words: herbal medicine and cosmetic cream need a manufacturing licence before sale.

## Misbranded and spurious drugs
Drugs not of standard quality or made under a false name are misbranded or spurious and punishable.
