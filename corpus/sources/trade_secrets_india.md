# Trade secrets

## Protection without registration
Indian law protects confidential business information through contract and the law of breach of confidence. In plain words: your secret recipe stays protected as long as you keep it secret and share it only under a non-disclosure agreement.

## Remedies for misuse
Courts grant injunctions and damages against persons who disclose or use confidential information in breach of confidence.
