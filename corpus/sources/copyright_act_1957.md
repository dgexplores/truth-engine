# Copyright

## Sec 13 — Works protected
Copyright subsists in original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works, cinematograph films and sound recordings. In plain words: your Ayurveda textbook text is protected as a literary work.

## Sec 52 — Fair dealing
Research, private study, criticism, review and reporting of current events do not constitute infringement.
