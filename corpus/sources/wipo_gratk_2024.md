# GRATK

## Art 3 — Disclosure of origin
Contracting parties shall require patent applicants to disclose the country of origin of genetic resources where the claimed invention is materially based on them. In plain words: selling abroad, the WIPO rule requires disclosing origin.

## Art 5 — Sanctions
Appropriate sanctions shall apply for failure to disclose as required under this Treaty.
