# FSSAI

## Food safety approval
No food business shall manufacture or sell any article of food without license. In plain words: food product selling needs FSSAI license and label rules.

## Packaging and labelling
Labels must declare veg/non-veg logo, ingredients, nutritional information and best-before date.
