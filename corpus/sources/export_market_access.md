# Export access

## EU herbal market
Ayurveda exports to the EU need THMPD registration with proof of 30 years traditional use, 15 within the EU. In plain words: selling herbal medicine in Europe needs traditional-use registration.

## US dietary supplements
US sales go under DSHEA as dietary supplements with good manufacturing practice and adverse-event reporting.
