# BDA

## Sec 6 — Prior approval for IPR
No person shall apply for any intellectual property right in or outside India for any invention based on any research or information on a biological resource obtained from India without obtaining prior approval of the National Biodiversity Authority.

## Sec 3 — Access approval
Certain persons must obtain prior approval of the Authority before accessing biological resources for commercial utilization. In plain words: you need permission to use a plant resource for a product.
