# Trademarks

## Sec 9 — Absolute grounds for refusal
A trade mark shall not be registered if it is devoid of distinctive character or consists of marks designating kind, quality, or geographical origin. In plain words: a generic brand name for selling goods cannot be trademarked.

## Sec 29 — Infringement
Use of an identical or deceptively similar mark in the course of trade amounts to infringement.
