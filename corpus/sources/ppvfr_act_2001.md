# PPVFR

## Sec 15 — Registrable varieties
New, distinct, uniform and stable plant varieties and farmers varieties can be registered. In plain words: a new seed variety bred by a farmer gets protection.

## Sec 39 — Farmers rights
Farmers may save, sow, and exchange seed as traditionally done.
